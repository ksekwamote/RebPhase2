Our Services

A comprehensive array of services crafted around your risk management needs.

From strategic consultancy to bespoke risk assessment, each service reflects REB’s dedication to empowering organisations with practical risk solutions.

01 · Risk Management

Enterprise Risk Management (ERM)

Comprehensive enterprise-wide risk assessments tailored to the business environment, supporting risk response strategies, frameworks, strategies and policies. REB’s iterative approach considers operations, business functions, projects and the wider industrial, micro and macro environment.

02 · Resilience

Business Continuity Management

A four-in-one programme covering Emergency Response, Crisis Communication, Disaster Recovery and Business Continuity Plans, informed by critical loss scenarios and business impact analysis.

03 · Engineering

Risk Engineering & Insurance Advisory Solutions

End-to-end risk assessments designed to identify material exposures, devise loss prevention and control strategies, improve safety, reduce claims and support informed risk financing.

04 · Sustainability

Environmental Social Governance (ESG) Framework & Reporting

Support for identifying material ESG issues, associated risks and opportunities, implementing frameworks and applying reporting standards in response to evolving sustainability expectations.

05 · Capability

Risk Management Training

Training programmes from foundational concepts to advanced strategies, designed to equip professionals with practical knowledge and embed risk-aware decision-making across the organisation.

06 · Insight

Integrated Risk Reporting Framework

Risk models and interactive dashboards that link risk profile, appetite, tolerance, strategy and performance, enabling scenario analysis and risk-adjusted planning.
